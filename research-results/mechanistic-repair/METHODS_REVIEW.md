# Independent methods review

## Evidence boundary

This review uses only the eight designated JSON files and the investigator-reported final English-instruction control and noun free-continuation factorial. Those final observations are supplied evidence, not independently rerun here. All 144 earlier discovery `op=patch` requests are excluded: the dispatch failure made them invalid, not negative results (`discovery/INVALIDATION.json`). Causal claims below use corrected `causal-v2/patch-effects.json`. Linguistic assessments remain provisional AI judgments, not human gold or calibrated quality scores.

## Available versus unexpressed capability

Correct clean-context replies demonstrate context-conditioned production, not reliable access to agreement in the failing context; clean learner text already supplies the desired numeral. In `discovery/diagnosis.json`, the correct complete book continuation has a teacher-forced log-probability advantage of **+0.688**, while the actual numeral fork favors the error (**−1.901** correct-versus-incorrect log-odds). Favorable full-phrase likelihood incorporates differently conditioned suffixes: it is neither the local greedy choice nor verified recognition.

The behavioral probes do not establish task-switched competence. Hebrew recognition selects B in all six counterbalanced orders; error editing copies all three errors. The reported English control likewise selects B in all six orders and copies both tested errors. Thus translation does not remove the position/copying confound. Preserving clean inputs is not evidence of correction. These failures neither prove hidden competence nor justify claiming the model lacks Hebrew (`causal-v2/behavioral-summary.json`).

The reported noun factorial narrows the dependency: both contexts produce ספרים after forced שני; clean + שתי still produces ספרים, whereas error + שתי produces גילידים. This supports context-dependent numeral–noun interaction, not deterministic corruption from שתי. Forced-prefix success supplies part of the answer and does not establish native original-context repair.

## Intervention versus mechanism

Corrected shams preserve all 12 recipient outputs. Shirt donor rescue occurs at five sites, but random-delta rescue occurs at four, including L12 where the donor fails. These counts are not independent replications; random-direction distributions and norm matching are not established. Moreover, shirt L34 random perturbation gives positive nominated-token odds but malformed free output.

Book numeral rescue occurs only at L34. Terminal donor/reverse margins reproduce their source-context preferences, consistent with importing an answer-bearing state rather than locating a native correction computation. Earlier donor and random interventions sometimes restore the noun without repairing agreement; reverse L34 restores the numeral error without the original noun corruption. These are separable outcomes, not a localized transferable repair (`discovery/post-patch-review.json`; `discovery/hypotheses.json`).

## Gates and disposition

- **C — not established:** no donor-specific, transferable cause with protected-case preservation. Retained prices alone are insufficient.
- **E — no verified weight repair:** the evidence is activation-only, an expressly permitted diagnostic label, not a verified linear-operator correction.
- **Q — not passed:** discovery judging reports 0 passes, 11 failures, and 13 unknowns; vocabulary is unassessed throughout. Other criterion minima are below nine. These single-turn judgments do not constitute full development evaluation (`discovery/quality-summary.json`).

Stopping speculative weight editing is warranted. No operator or indexed weight slice is implicated; no broad 9/10 claim follows. Under `research/mechanistic-repair/protocol.json`, promotion and final evaluation remain blocked. The appropriate disposition is **inconclusive about a repairable native cause**, not verified repair.

## At most two next investigations

1. A fixed, small format-control battery separating answer-position bias and copying from grammatical discrimination, with counterbalancing and independently checkable instruction-following controls.
2. Contingent on informative controls, bounded original-context contribution attribution at the book numeral/noun decisions, requiring multiple norm-matched random directions, sham/reverse controls, and protected-case transfer before considering any weight slice.
