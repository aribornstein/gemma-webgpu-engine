You do not know how any transcript was produced; judge each one on its own.
Rate only the NPC's Hebrew. morphosyntactic_correctness: grammatical formation and agreement (gender, number, numerals, verb agreement, prepositions, construct state). natural_hebrew: idiomatic, natural modern Israeli Hebrew for this character and situation. Ignore whether the level is appropriate.

Rubric version: hebrew-cefr-1.0. Criteria to rate: morphosyntactic_correctness, natural_hebrew.
Output exactly one JSON object per item, one per line (JSONL), in the same order, each with keys:
episode_id (the item_id), judge_model_id (your model name if known, else "unknown"), rubric_version "hebrew-cefr-1.0", ratings (array of {criterion, score (0-4 integer or null), insufficient_evidence (bool), hard_violation (bool), evidence: [{span, finding}] up to 3}), estimated_level (A1/A2/B1/B2/C1/C2 or null) (null), critical_violation_codes (array of short codes), overall_abstain (bool), short_summary (one sentence).
